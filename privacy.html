Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy describes how StreamHighs ("we") handles data in the StreamHighs Clipper application ("the app").

Who uses the app

The app is an internal tool. The only users are members of the StreamHighs team, who connect social media accounts that they own.

Data we collect

  • Account data from TikTok and Instagram after you authorize the app: your user ID, display name, username and avatar URL.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Instagram, used solely to upload or publish content on your behalf.
  • Content data: the video files and captions you choose to publish, and the resulting post IDs and links.

We do not collect data about other people, your contacts, your messages or your browsing.

How we use data

Only to upload videos to the connected accounts, check the status of those uploads and keep a record of what was published. We do not sell, share or use data for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

Tokens and records are stored on a computer controlled by the StreamHighs team. Video files are temporarily placed on cloud storage only for the time needed for Instagram to fetch them and are deleted afterwards.

Third parties

Data is sent only to TikTok (TikTok Content Posting API) and Meta (Instagram Platform API) to perform publishing, under their respective terms.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions) or Instagram settings (Apps and websites). To request deletion of any stored data, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
