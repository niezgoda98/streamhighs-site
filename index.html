StreamHighs Clipper

StreamHighs publishes short highlight clips from live streams on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

StreamHighs Clipper is an internal tool used only by the StreamHighs team. It prepares clips and uploads them to the team's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API, the Instagram Platform API and the YouTube Data API. It is not available to third parties.
